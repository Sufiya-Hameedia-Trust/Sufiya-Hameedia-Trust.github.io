Message from the President

Dr. Nasim Akhtar

President, SUFIYA HAMEEDIA TRUST

Greetings from the President's Desk,

As we stand to begin the new academic session, I the President of SUFIYA HAMEEDIA TRUST and the principal of SUFIYA HAMEEDIA Group of Institutions extend a hearty and warm welcome to all my students, staff and parents.

Each academic year is a new height scaled; new targets set for innovation in the future at all institutions. The preaching, teaching and the learning legacy of "ASHAB-E-SUFFA" and the 'SUFIYA-E-KARAM', set as example by one of our scholar Qutub-E-Banaras 'Asshah Abdul Hameed Alai-Hir-Rahmah' is followed as an identity of Ahle-Sunnnat-Wa-Jamat at Sufiya Hameedia Group of Institutions.

We cater the need of all the age group of learners by fostering their individual creativity, social skills, Islamic (Deeni) and academic intellect and provide the most conducive environment for all round growth and development of our student's and the community.

We are on the path to achieve 100% literacy as the goal of our National Education Policy (NEP-2020) aligned with Global Education Agenda, particularly the Sustainable Development Goal 4 (SDG-4).
