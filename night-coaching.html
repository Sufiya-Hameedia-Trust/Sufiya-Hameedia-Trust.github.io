About Our Institution

This is a happy moment to announce that admissions are open in the SUFIYA HAMEEDIA Night Coaching Classes. Night coaching has been running for several years and was upgraded as 'A Unit of Sufiya Hameedia Continuing Education Section' on the first day of June 2023 (12th of Zil-Qadah, 1444) as an institute for lifelong learning under the auspices of 'SUFIYA HAMEEDIA TRUST'.

This initiative strengthens the 'NATIONAL EDUCATIONAL POLICY 2020' (NEP-2020) detailed at Chapter 21: Adult Education and Life Long Learning. The vision of the centre is to achieve 100% literacy with a mission to provide education for every age group of candidates for lifelong learning.

Who We Serve

SUFIYA HAMEEDIA Night Coaching Classes addresses all those students who could not complete their formal education and achieved adulthood but having the burning zeal to be graduated. As these groups of candidates are busy in their earning for family in the day time, separate coaching classes are conducted in the night between 9:00pm – 11:00pm for every individual learners of any age group.

The Continuing Education Section also covers illiterates and adults with very low literacy to adopt and make them ready for the current digital World.

Admission Process

Any candidate seeking admission may visit the institution or call us. The student will be examined by the admission coordinator and asked for an admission test. Based on performance, the coordinator will decide the standards for admission to appropriate level.

Office will issue an application form and help to fill all the information correctly as per the documents submitted.

Learning Methods & Coordination

The centre coordinates with Directorate of Adult Education (DAE) of Adult Education Bureau, a subordinate office under the Department of School Education & Literacy, Ministry of Education, Government of India, Shastri Bhawan, New Delhi, India.

Special Programs

For Underprivileged Students

All those underprivileged students who are not able to continue their formal education can continue learning under open schooling and open-university system.

For Adult Learners

All those grownups and adults who have crossed the age of formal education and wish to continue learning will be groomed to do so through individualized learning through coaching to appear for open schooling and/or open-university examination system for certificate and degree.

Individual Tutoring

Another mission of the centre is to have provisions for tutoring weak students and slow learners through individualized tuition classes for them.
