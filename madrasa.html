Sufiya Hameedia Arabic Madrasa

(Islamic Education with Modern Approach)

Mission: To provide Quranic education for every age groups of candidate for lifelong learning.

Sufiya Hameedia is a place of learning for Muslims community lined on the concept of "ASHAB-E-SUFFA" and based on the teaching of 'SUFIYA-E-KARAM'. The preaching, teaching and learning legacy of one of the scholar Qutub-E-Banaras 'Asshah Abdul Hameed Alai-Hir-Rahmah' is followed as an identity of Ahle-Sunnnat-Wa-Jamat of Banaras in Bangalore.

Our Islamic Education Programs

SUFIYA HAMEEDIA Arabic Madrasa follow Hafiz-e-Quran, Dars-e-Nizami, Nazrah Quran with Tajweed and other Islamic practices to cater the need of those students who find free hours in the morning and evening. We are planning comprehensive curriculum expansion in near future.

We feel happy and elated to share that SUFIYA HAMEEDIA Arabic Madrasa came into existence by your hard work and your generous contributions, and is now running successfully to enhance and improve the educational literacy of Muslim community.

Current Programs & Achievements

There are 29 students who are at different stages of Hifz-e-Quran. On an average about 4-5 students gets Hifz certificate every year and established the Tarawih in the month of Ramadhan.

There are 36 students in Nazra section following Quranic recitation with Tajweed. About 5 students are admitted to the Aalim course taught by qualified Aalim, Fazil and Hafiz.

Future Plans

We are planning to follow a comprehensive curriculum with TAHTANIYA, MOLVI, AALIM and FAZILAT as well as a Secondary English Medium School that requires significant contributions from the community.

Community Support & Contributions

Today we have a modest facility with a few classrooms to accommodate the existing students. We are running short of classrooms and face financial challenges to meet the expenses. To improve further, our expansion plans require community support.

Sufiya Hameedia Trust® manages the three educational institutions completely based on your generous contributions. We request your continued support through Atiya, Sadaqa, Zakat, Fitra and every contribution directly to trust bank account.

We are inviting you to become an active member of the 'SUFIYA HAMEEDIA TRUST' to serve the community for their educational needs. Let us join together for a better tomorrow.

Supporting Underprivileged Students

All those underprivileged students who are not able to continue their formal education can continue learning under open schooling and open-university system.
